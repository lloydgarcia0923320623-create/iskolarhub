03 BUILD NOTES

Thoughtful in
the details.

Some of the engineering decisions behind IskolarHub—from access boundaries to the experience when you're offline.

See how to run it ↗
  1. 01

    Offline-aware experience

    Locally cached scholarship records and checklist data keep useful details available without a connection.

    ◎
  2. 02

    Role-based administration

    Administrator tools rely on server-managed Firebase claims, rather than student-controlled profile data.

    ◇
  3. 03

    AI with clear limits

    Guidance uses retrieved in-app materials and doesn't claim to decide eligibility or award outcomes.

THE IMPORTANT BIT

Honest engineering includes honest limitations.

IskolarHub is a prototype. Its preliminary matching only compares structured information in the app; it does not confirm eligibility or guarantee an award. Students should verify current requirements with each provider and apply through its official channel.
