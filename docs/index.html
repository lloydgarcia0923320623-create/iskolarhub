Finding a scholarship is one thing.
Keeping it all together is another.

Between searching for opportunities, understanding the requirements, and tracking documents, it can be hard to see what comes next. IskolarHub brings those pieces into one student-support experience.

THE IMPORTANT BIT

Helpful guidance. No false promises.

IskolarHub is a prototype. Matching is preliminary, based only on structured information recorded in the app. It does not confirm eligibility or guarantee an award. Always check current requirements and apply through the scholarship provider's official channel.
